# BitScribe Steward - Quick Start Guide

Welcome to BitScribe Steward! This brief guide will help you get up and running quickly so you can start auditing and cataloging your media library.

## Step 1: Add Folders to Scan
1. Open BitScribe Steward.
2. Navigate to the **Media Scan & Report Export** dashboard (the default screen).
3. Under the "Scan Folders" section, click to browse and select the directories containing your movies, TV shows, or music. 
4. You can add multiple top-level folders to your scan queue.

## Step 2: Run the Scan
1. Once your folders are added, click the **Start Scan** button.
2. BitScribe will begin analyzing your files. It reads metadata safely without modifying any files.
3. You can watch the progress bar as the engine probes your files for codecs, bitrates, subtitles, and potential corruption.

## Step 3: View Your Results
When the scan finishes, you have two primary ways to explore your library:

*   **Analyze Dashboard**: Click the **Analyze** tab to see visual charts, health metrics, and instant alerts for duplicates or corrupted files.
*   **Library Dashboard**: Click the **Library** tab to browse a highly searchable and sortable list of every media item found. You can filter by category, compatibility level, or anomaly status.

## Step 4: Export Reports
If you want to share your library or analyze it in another tool:
1. Return to the **Media Scan & Report Export** dashboard.
2. Locate the **Export Reports** section.
3. Choose your desired format (Excel, HTML Web Report, CSV, or JSON).
4. Save the file to your computer.

## Step 5: Customize Your Rules (Optional)
If you are optimizing your media for a specific home server (like Plex) or TV:
1. Go to the **Options** dashboard.
2. Adjust the compatibility toggles to define what codecs, bitrates, and resolutions are "Direct Play" friendly for your setup. 
3. Your library's compatibility scores will automatically update based on your new rules!
