### Fix Product Tour "Show Demo" Auto-Advance Bug
- **Problem**: When clicking the "Show Demo" button on the Tour Remote Control (e.g., for the Metrics Filter Mode on slide 9), the tour would unexpectedly advance to the next slide immediately instead of playing the demo. This occurred because `useTourSimulation` triggers native `.click()` events on DOM elements (like the filter buttons) inside the Joyride `target` area. Even after `react-joyride` was commanded to pause (`run={false}`), it maintained its native click listeners on the target element and interpreted the simulated button click as a user click on the target, prompting it to emit a `step:after` (action: 'next') event, which immediately skipped the demo and advanced the slide.
- **Solution**: Updated `handleJoyrideCallback` in `useAppTour.ts` and `ProductTour.tsx` to immediately discard all Joyride callbacks if `activeDemo !== null`. Added explicit `type="button"`, `e.preventDefault()`, and `e.stopPropagation()` to the buttons inside `TourRemoteControl.tsx` to prevent any native form or bubbling side-effects.
